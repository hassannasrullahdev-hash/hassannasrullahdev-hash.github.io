Privacy Policy for Overtake

Last updated 4 November 2026

Overtake is a racing game published by Datrix Labs. This policy explains, plainly, what the game does and does not do with information. It is short because the game does very little.

The game asks you for nothing

There is no account, no sign-in and no registration. Overtake never asks for your name, your email address, your phone number or your location, and it has no way to send any of those anywhere.

Your progress stays on your phone

Your coins, the cars and roads you have unlocked, your best distance and your settings are written to a single file inside the app's own private storage on your device. That file is never uploaded, never backed up to us, and is removed when you uninstall the game. We cannot read it and we never receive a copy of it.

Advertising

Overtake shows ads through Google AdMob, which is the only third-party service in the game. To choose and measure ads, Google may receive information from your device, including its advertising ID, device and app details, and an approximate location worked out from your IP address. That information goes to Google, not to us: we receive only totals, such as how many ads were shown, with nothing in them that identifies a person.

How Google uses that data is described in Google's Privacy & Terms. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Nothing else is collected

Overtake contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no social network connection, no tracking pixels and no other advertising network. It makes no network requests of its own.

Children

Overtake is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, write to the address below and we will look into it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date at the top. A change that affects what leaves your device will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
